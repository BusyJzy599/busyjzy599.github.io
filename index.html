04 About the space

Research
direction.

My work sits at the intersection of computational pathology, multimodal learning, and clinical translation.

I am currently studying how gliomas respond to treatment, how models should reason when modalities are missing, and how spatial transcriptomics can connect tissue organization with molecular programs.

The broader goal is to make multimodal AI more useful in practice: robust to incomplete observations, explicit about uncertainty, and grounded in the biology that clinicians need to see.
